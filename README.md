About

GiantDays-CE is an unofficial community continuation of the GiantDays tileset for Cataclysm, originally created by snw-0 and merged into the official tileset repository on August 8, 2023.

GiantDays builds upon the foundations of NeoDays and RetroDays, retaining their distinctive retro pixel art aesthetic while expanding the tileset to a 20×20 resolution.

The original creator acknowledged that some assets, particularly character and equipment overlays, remained unfinished at the time of submission.

GiantDays-CE aims to continue that work.

Our Philosophy

This project is not intended to redesign, replace, or improve upon the original artist's vision. Its purpose is to continue the unfinished work while preserving what makes GiantDays unique.

The original artists deserve full recognition for their creativity, dedication, and the considerable amount of work that went into developing these tilesets.

Project Goals

Correct misaligned clothing and equipment overlays.

Address sprite positioning and scaling inconsistencies.

Resolve unfinished character overlays.

Preserve the original pixel art, proportions, and visual identity.

Maintain compatibility with supported Cataclysm branches.

Encourage community feedback and contributions.

Current Progress

Initial equipment overlay corrections have demonstrated promising results across numerous character professions and clothing combinations.

Some individual sprites still require attention. This remains a work in progress, and complete compatibility is not yet guaranteed.

AI Transparency

ChatGPT has been used to assist with technical analysis, scripting, configuration modifications, and sprite alignment corrections.

Changes are reviewed and tested in-game by the project maintainer.

No AI-generated artwork is used or accepted in this project.

GiantDays-CE is committed to preserving human-created pixel art and respecting the original artists' work.

Compatibility

The project aims to support:

Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead (CDDA)

Cataclysm: Bright Nights (CBN)

Cataclysm: The Last Generation (CTLG)

Compatibility will be tested individually and documented as development progresses.

Community Contributions

Bug reports, screenshots, suggestions, and contributions are welcome.

When reporting a visual issue, please include the affected item or sprite, your Cataclysm branch and version, and a screenshot demonstrating the problem whenever possible.
